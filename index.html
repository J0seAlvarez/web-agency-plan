Design best practices

Strategy before pixels, restraint around one signature moment.

  • Strategy before pixels One primary goal and one CTA per page
  • Strong hierarchy Bold fluid type scale, generous whitespace, 8pt spacing grid
  • Design system first Tokens for color, type, spacing, radius and motion in Figma variables, mirrored in code
  • One signature moment per site Hero interaction, scroll story or custom illustration; restraint everywhere else
  • Motion with purpose 150–400 ms, ease-out, honor reduced-motion settings
  • Real content early No lorem ipsum past wireframes
  • Budget for photography and art direction AI imagery for concepting or under tight brand control
  • Mobile-first Design at 375, 768, 1280 and 1920 px
